Birgit, a member of the ISE staff, shared a career path that didn't start with software engineering as the
destination. It wasn't her first calling, but the skills it gave her let her pursue and grow the work she really cared
about.

Her career has been driven by research and by the impact of the work, which I found really interesting. It was a
reminder that software engineering can be a tool for whatever you care about, not just a job title.
