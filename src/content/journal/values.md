### Accountability

If I say I'll do something, I do it, and if something goes wrong on my watch, I own it. Teams only work when people
can rely on each other, and I want to be someone a team can count on.

**Where I've lived it:** on the forecourt at Prior Park Service Station, I'm responsible for handling transactions
correctly and making sure safety guidelines are followed. Customers and colleagues rely on me to get it right.

### Honesty

I'd rather give an honest answer, even an unwelcome one, than a convenient one. Honest feedback is how teams catch
problems early, and it's the basis of trust with colleagues, clients and anyone you might one day pitch to.

**Where I've lived it:** as an exam attendant at CBS High School Clonmel, my job was to make sure every exam rule and
procedure was followed strictly. The whole point of the role is integrity: the results only mean something if the
process is fair and honest.

### Equality

Everyone deserves a fair chance and to be heard, whatever their background or experience. The best ideas don't always
come from the loudest or most senior person in the room.

**Where I've lived it:** as an [assistant coach for underage teams at Clonmel Commercials](/journal/beyond-the-classroom#coaching-clonmel-commercials), giving every player a fair
go, not just the strongest ones, was part of the job.

### Why they matter for where I'm going

Residencies will put me into new teams with new people, and the startup I'm working on with Fionn depends entirely on
trust between the two of us. Being accountable, honest and fair is how I want to earn that trust, wherever I end up.
