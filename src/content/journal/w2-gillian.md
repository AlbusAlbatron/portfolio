Gillian told us about the interesting path that led her to ISE, then turned the session into a hands-on lesson in
project management. Working in teams, we had to build a spaghetti tower on a budget and under time pressure.

It was a lot of fun, and our team won best tower. More than that, it taught me a lot about working in a team,
especially when the clock is running.
