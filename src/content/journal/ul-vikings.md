### What I do

In September 2026 I joined the UL Vikings, the university's American football team, where I play running back on
offence. I'd never played American football before, but I came in with years of school football and basketball
behind me, and a lot of that carried over. I train with the team regularly and get involved in club events and
socials.

> There's a certain feeling you only get knowing you're about to run as hard as possible through a D-line that
> really wants to hit you.

### What it's done for me

- **Teamwork and communication.** Every play only works if everyone does their job, and that depends on
  communicating clearly and quickly.
- **Resilience.** Learning a new, physical sport from scratch means getting things wrong, taking hits and getting
  back up.
- **Belonging.** It's given me a group of friends and a place at UL beyond my course.
- **Time management.** Fitting training around the ISE timetable and a part-time job has made me much more
  deliberate about how I use my week.
