### What I do

When classmates get stuck, I help where I can: debugging code together, explaining a concept that hasn't clicked
yet, running study sessions before labs and deadlines, and sharing notes, useful links and setup guides. In group
projects I try to make sure nobody stays blocked for long. Most of it is programming in Java, Python and C, and
getting tools set up, like Git, GitHub and Linux.

### The impact

Classmates get unstuck and finish their work faster than they would on their own, and our group work runs more
smoothly because nobody is left behind.

### What it's done for me

Explaining something is the best test of whether I really understand it. Helping others has made my own
understanding of the material much stronger, and it's practice at explaining technical ideas clearly, which is a
big part of working in any engineering team.
