ISE's business development lead had a career story unlike any other we've heard so far. He studied business and
psychology and came up through sales rather than engineering. Three points stood out to me:

- The best interviews you'll ever sit are the ones you network your way into.
- Your personal brand is worth working on.
- Whenever you pitch something, whether it's a product to investors or yourself in an interview, it has to solve a
  problem for the person you're pitching to and fit their situation.

**What I'm taking away:** treat every interview as a pitch. Start from what the employer needs, and show how I fit.
