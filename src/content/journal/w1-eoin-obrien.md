Our first CareersLab session opened with Eoin O'Brien walking us through his career as an entrepreneur. It's an
impressive track record, and it was obvious from the way he talked about it how sharp he is.

The timing couldn't have been better. My classmate Fionn and I are in the early stages of our own startup, so his
practical advice on getting a venture off the ground was directly useful to us, especially on how to go about
raising capital and how much of the early work comes down to building the right connections.

**What I'm taking away:** if we reach the point of looking for funding for our startup, this is the talk I'll be
coming back to.
