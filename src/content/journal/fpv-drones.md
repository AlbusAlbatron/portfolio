### What I do

I've been flying FPV (first-person view) drones for a year or two. I build them myself from parts, and when one
doesn't behave, I work out why and fix it. I mostly fly for the simple reason that it genuinely feels like flying, and
I'm especially into freestyle.

I also crash them, which I enjoy a lot less. Then I take them apart and repair them, and get back in the air.

### What it's done for me

- **Hands-on hardware.** Building drones from parts balances out all the time I spend writing software.
- **Debugging in the real world.** Tracking down a fault in a physical machine is a different kind of debugging from
  finding a bug in code, and I've learned a lot from doing both.
- **Persistence.** Crash, repair, fly again. Freestyle is a skill you only build by getting it wrong a lot.
