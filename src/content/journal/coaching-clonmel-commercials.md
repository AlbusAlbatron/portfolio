Before coming to UL, I spent a season as an assistant coach with some of the youngest Gaelic football players at
Clonmel Commercials, my home club.

### What I did

I ran drills and warm-ups at training, and looked after the practical side: equipment, setting up and making sure
sessions could run smoothly.

### What it taught me

- **Patience and leadership.** Keeping a group of young players focused and enjoying themselves takes patience, and
  it taught me a lot about leading a group.
- **Fairness.** Every child deserved the same time and attention, not just the strongest players. It's where my
  value of [equality](/journal/learn-more#values) comes from in practice.
- **Responsibility.** Parents were trusting me with their kids, and being a good role model mattered as much as any
  drill I ran.
