In our second lecture, George from 3M spoke about getting new products off the ground. His central message was to
keep thinking radically, and not to let a large organisation grind you down into mediocrity.

He told us about one of his favourite 3M products, one that still failed despite the team doing everything right.
Some things are simply out of your control. It clearly didn't put him off, and that willingness to take on risk came
through in everything he said.

**What I'm taking away:** I don't have to choose between working for a big company and keeping my creativity.
Wherever I end up, startup or multinational, I want to hold on to my own ideas and my own way of thinking.
