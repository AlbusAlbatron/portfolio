### Biography

I'm a first-year Immersive Software Engineering student at the University of Limerick, from Clonmel, Co. Tipperary.
I mostly work in Java, Python and C, and outside of class I build my own projects, from a small physics engine to a
distraction blocker and a procedural animation experiment ([see my work](/projects)). I've also worked part-time on a busy service station forecourt, as an exam attendant during the State Examinations, and in
a hotel restaurant.

### Key strengths

- **Problem-solving:** I enjoy algorithmic problems and practise them in my spare time.
- **Building things end to end:** my projects go from an idea to working software.
- **Reliability:** part-time jobs in busy, customer-facing roles where people depend on you to turn up and get it right.
- **Teamwork under pressure:** most recently, a winning spaghetti tower in a timed project management challenge.

### Career interests

- Software engineering residencies that give me a wide range of industry experience.
- Entrepreneurship: my classmate Fionn and I are in the early stages of our own startup.
