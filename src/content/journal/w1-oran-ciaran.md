Two third-year ISE students, Oran and Ciarán, gave us honest advice on the residencies ahead of us.

Their main point was to go after a wide range of industry experience rather than simply chasing the highest-paying
residency. The real goal is a job after graduation, not what you earn as an intern, and the breadth of experience is
what gets you there. They also gave us a candid look at how office politics work, and how different that can be in
a large multinational compared with a tiny startup.

**What I'm taking away:** when it comes to choosing residencies, I'll weigh what I'll learn and the variety of
experience ahead of the salary.
